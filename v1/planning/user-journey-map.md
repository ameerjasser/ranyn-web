# User Journey Map - Ranyn Labs

This document charts how different user personas discover and interact with the Ranyn Labs website.

## Persona 1: Enterprise Client (Sarah, CTO)
- **Objective**: Find a reliable partner to deploy a secure, high-performance ML model pipeline.
- **Journey**:
  1. **Discovery**: Lands on home page from a recommendation or LinkedIn post.
  2. **First Impression**: Captivated by clean, expressive UI & tagline (Ethical Engineering).
  3. **Verification**: Navigates to the Interactive Labs terminal to see raw engineering capability and studies the expertise cards.
  4. **Action**: Submits an inquiry via the Contact form.

## Persona 2: Research Talent (Alex, AI Student/Researcher)
- **Objective**: Explore job/research opportunities and learn about Ranyn's work.
- **Journey**:
  1. **Discovery**: Finds Ranyn Labs on LinkedIn while searching for neural network roles.
  2. **First Impression**: Views modern mono typography (IBM Plex Mono) reflecting a developer-friendly culture.
  3. **Verification**: Inspects the team highlights and current research topics (AI Image Processing, Neural Networks).
  4. **Action**: Clicks call-to-action button or copies the contact info to apply.

## Persona 3: Tech Partner (Marcus, Investor)
- **Objective**: Assess the technical viability and ethical posture of the labs.
- **Journey**:
  1. **Discovery**: Press release or LinkedIn post.
  2. **First Impression**: Clean dark-mode aesthetic with vibrant accent hues representing premium tech.
  3. **Verification**: Reads the core philosophy on the home page about "Intelligent Systems. Ethical Engineering."
  4. **Action**: Connects directly via email or LinkedIn links.
