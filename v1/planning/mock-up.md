## Key Mock-up Interactions
- **Terminal Console**: Simulates terminal outputs when hovering over or loading the page.
- **Button Hover States**: Highlight borders with a smooth neon glow transition.
- **Responsive Layout**: Adapts gracefully to screen resolutions ranging from 320px wide up to 2560px.
