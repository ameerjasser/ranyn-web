Fellowship

A twelve-week programme for students and recent graduates. Choose a research question or an industry project and work with a mentor from day one.

Compare tracks ↓Programme PDF ↗

Two tracks

Research

Review the literature, design experiments and revise a paper with a mentor. Topics include computer vision, robotics navigation, machine learning and AI security.

Industry

Build and maintain features in AI agents, conversational systems or healthcare tools. Take a feature through code review, deployment and monitoring.

Who can apply?

Final-year students and recent graduates in computer science, AI, data science or a related field. Both tracks require Python, Git, data structures and algorithms, Linux command line and technical writing. The programme PDF lists the additional skills for each track.

Twelve weeks with a mentor

  1. Weeks 1–2 · Meet your mentor and scope the project.
  2. Weeks 3–4 · Set up the research pipeline or pick up the first feature.
  3. Weeks 5–6 · Run experiments or build the feature end to end.
  4. Weeks 7–8 · Revise with mentor and code review.
  5. Weeks 9–10 · Draft the paper or ship and monitor the feature.
  6. Weeks 11–12 · Finish your work and present to the team.

Prepare an application

Include your track preference and why, latest qualification, relevant experience, certifications or courses, academic transcript, project proposal, statement of purpose and portfolio link or snapshots.

Email hr@ranynlabs.com. The programme asks applicants to allow up to two weeks for a response. Selected applicants receive an invitation to an initial call.

For current intake details, write to fellowship@ranynlabs.com.
